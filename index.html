Nicola Canale

Experimental physicist at the University of Ferrara and INFN Ferrara. I build instrumentation and data acquisition systems, from particle detectors to optics for gravitational wave observatories.

Work

My research runs across three areas that share the same underlying craft: measuring small signals reliably. I work on particle physics with BESIII, on nuclear physics and electric dipole moment searches with JEDI, and on the optical characterisation of substrates for the Einstein Telescope.

Outside the lab, I am national vice-president of Pint of Science Italia, which brings researchers into pubs to talk about what they actually do.
